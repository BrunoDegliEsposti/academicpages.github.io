My research goal is to develop a purely meshless pipeline for the numerical
solution of partial differential equations on 3D CAD geometries based on the
original combination of the following techniques:
meshless boundary methods, possibly coupled with volume methods such as RBF-FD,
my own C++ library [NodeGenLib](https://github.com/BrunoDegliEsposti/NodeGenLib)
for the generation of variable-density
unstructured nodes on the surface of 3D CAD domains given in B-Rep format, the
[moment-free meshless quadrature formulas](/publication/2025-07-18-meshless-moment-free-quadrature)
introduced in my doctoral dissertation, and methods such as
[quadrature by expansion](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2013.06.027)
that allow singular integrals to be evaluated in terms of regular integrals.
