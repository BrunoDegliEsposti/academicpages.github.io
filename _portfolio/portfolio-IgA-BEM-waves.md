My research goal is to advance numerical schemes for the simulation of
3D acoustic and elastic wave propagation phenomena by designing an efficient,
high-order Isogeometric Boundary Element Method (IgA-BEM) framework
based on convolution quadrature.
Novel strategies for the accurate computation of collocation BEM integrals
will be investigated in the case of piecewise smooth multi-patch CAD geometries,
possibly with trimming.
