I am a postdoctoral researcher (assegnista di ricerca) in numerical analysis
at the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science
*Ulisse Dini* of the University of Florence, Italy,
under the supervision of Prof. Alessandra Sestini.

I received my PhD in mathematics in March 2025 through a cotutelle program
between the University of Florence and Justus-Liebig-Universität Gießen,
graduating *summa cum laude* under the supervision of Prof. Alessandra Sestini
(Florence) and Prof. Dr. Oleg Davydov (Gießen, Germany).
My [dissertation](/publication/2025-03-21-doctoral-dissertation)
addresses two fundamental challenges in meshless numerical
methods: robust node generation for complex domains,
including 3D CAD geometries, and high-order numerical integration
on scattered nodes.

More information about my academic journey is available in my [CV](/cv/).

# Research interests

* Isogeometric Analysis combined with the Boundary Element Method (IgA-BEM)
* High-order quadrature formulas on scattered (unstructured) nodes
* Meshless domain discretization of 3D CAD geometries. I am the developer
of [NodeGenLib](https://github.com/BrunoDegliEsposti/NodeGenLib/), a C++
library for node generation using the advancing front method
* Stable meshless schemes for advection-dominated PDEs
* High-performance and parallel computing (HPC)
