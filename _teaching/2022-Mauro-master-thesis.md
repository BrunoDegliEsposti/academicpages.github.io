Candidate: [Mauro Giliberti](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Tfyb8n0AAAAJ).
Advisors: Prof. Aldo Lorenzo Cotrone, Dr. Francesco Bigazzi.
Title of the thesis: *Numerical solution of bubble
dynamics in holographic vacuum decay*.
The thesis is part of a collaboration between the department of physics
and the department of mathematics of the university of Florence.
I have introduced Mauro Giliberti to numerical tools such as quadrature
formulas, spline curves, splines on triangulations, and nonlinear optimization methods.
Final grade: 110/110 with honors.
