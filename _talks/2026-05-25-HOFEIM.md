Best poster award, 2nd place, at the
10th International Workshop on High-Order
Finite Element and Isogeometric Methods (HOFEIM2026).
Links to the workshop [webpage](https://sites.google.com/unifi.it/hofeim2026/),
to the [poster](/files/poster-HOFEIM-Siena.pdf),
and to an [animation](/files/poster-HOFEIM-animation.mp4)
of the numerical solution of the homogeneous wave equation around a toy boat
from t = 0 to t = 10 with 718 spatial DOFs and 50 timesteps.

The manufactured Neumann boundary condition is given by the time convolution of
the fundamental solution with a (causal) sinusoidal signal.
Cubic splines are used for both the space and time discretizations.
Wave speed c is equal to 1.
